Raseed reads the SMS your bank sends you, turns each one into a transaction, and groups your spending by month and category — entirely on the device.

No account. No server. No sync. No telemetry. The app does not even request the INTERNET permission, and the build fails if it ever does.

• Only sender IDs you allow are ever read; everything else is dropped before it is stored.
• Parsing is template matching over the bank's own labels (مبلغ:, لدى:, بطاقة:) — no cloud, no model.
• Categories come from a merchant dictionary plus your own corrections, which persist. When unsure it says "Uncategorized" rather than guessing.
• Database encrypted with SQLCipher; key held in the Android Keystore; excluded from OS backup.
• Encrypted backup file you keep wherever you like.
• Arabic and English.

Saudi banks first (AlRajhi, Alinma, SNB, SABB, Al Bilad, STC Pay, …). Templates and the merchant dictionary are open data — contribute your bank.
